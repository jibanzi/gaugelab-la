Shipping Policy

Tachi Club ships only to street addresses in the 50 US states and Washington, D.C. Shipping times, tracking, lost packages and address rules.

Last updated 2026-11-01.

Where do you ship?

We ship only to street addresses in the 50 US states and Washington, D.C. (including Alaska and Hawaii).

Where don't you ship?

We do not ship to PO Boxes, APO/FPO/DPO military or diplomatic addresses, Puerto Rico or other US territories, or any address outside the United States. Orders with these addresses are refused at checkout.

How much is shipping?

US shipping is calculated at checkout based on your ZIP and package size; free local pickup at our Los Angeles warehouse. Items that ship from our Los Angeles warehouse (digital clusters and made-to-order parts): enter your ZIP on the order form and we show the shipping amount as its own line before you pay; it is based on the actual packed size and weight and a live carrier rate from Los Angeles. Deposit orders pay shipping together with the balance; pay-in-full orders pay it at checkout. Choose "Local pickup at our LA warehouse" for $0 shipping; pickup details are emailed after you order. In-stock items that ship directly from our supplier (marked "In stock · pay in full") include US shipping in the price.

When will my order ship? (in-stock items)

In-stock accessories (marked "In stock · pay in full") ship directly from our supplier, usually within 2–5 business days after your order. Transit is usually about 7–15 business days.

When will my order ship? (custom items)

Custom / made-to-order items ship after production, per the lead time shown on each product page: digital clusters are estimated at 3–5 weeks, and other custom parts at about 2–6 weeks (large items such as the 718 rear diffuser 4–6 weeks). We email you if the estimate changes.

Signature confirmation

Orders with a goods value of $500 or more ship with signature confirmation required on delivery (adult signature where the carrier offers it). Please make sure someone can sign for the package.

Tracking and delivery confirmation

When your order ships we email you the carrier and tracking number. When the carrier shows it delivered, we email you a delivery confirmation.

Late or lost packages

If tracking has not updated or your package has not arrived, email support@oliandcece.com with your order number and we will open a trace with the carrier. If there is still no delivery scan 30 days after shipment, we treat the package as lost and send a replacement or a refund.

Wrong address

Please check your address at checkout; you are responsible for a correct, complete address. Before shipment, email support@oliandcece.com and we will try to update it. After shipment we cannot change it. If a package is returned or lost because of an incorrect address, reshipping may cost extra.

Damaged, wrong or missing items

If your item arrives damaged, is the wrong item, or is missing parts, email support@oliandcece.com with your order number and photos within 7 days of delivery. We send a replacement first, or refund you if a replacement is not possible. This is the only exception to the no-returns rule for shipped orders.

Contact

Email support@oliandcece.com with your order number.
