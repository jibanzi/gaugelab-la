Returns & Refund Policy

Cancellation and refund rules for Tachi Club custom orders (full refund before you confirm production start; after that, refund minus actual costs incurred, capped at the deposit) and in-stock items (full refund before shipment); 7-day damaged/wrong item cover.

Last updated 2026-11-01.

Production-start confirmation (custom items)

After you pay for a custom item, we email you a summary of your order (vehicle, VIN, options, lead time) with a Confirm production start link. We do not order parts or start production until you click it. The time, IP address and policy version of your confirmation are recorded with your order.

Deposit pre-orders (custom items)

Custom items are made/sourced to order. After your deposit is paid we email you an order summary (vehicle, VIN, options, lead time) with a Confirm production start link; we do not order parts or start production until you confirm. Cancel before you confirm production start: full refund. Cancel after production/purchasing has started: we refund your deposit minus the actual costs already incurred for your order (materials, machining, freight), with an itemized cost breakdown; the deduction never exceeds your deposit. Once shipped: no returns, refunds or exchanges. Exception: if your item arrives damaged, wrong or missing parts, email support@oliandcece.com with photos within 7 days of delivery; we send a replacement first, or refund you if a replacement is not possible. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method in 5–10 business days.

Paid-in-full custom items

PAY-IN-FULL refund policy (custom / made-to-order items): after payment we email you an order summary with a Confirm production start link; nothing is ordered or produced until you confirm. Cancel before you confirm: full refund. Cancel after production/purchasing has started: we refund the amount paid minus the actual costs already incurred (materials, machining, freight), with an itemized cost breakdown; the deduction never exceeds the deposit amount for that item. Once shipped: no returns, refunds or exchanges. Exception: if your item arrives damaged, wrong or missing parts, email support@oliandcece.com with photos within 7 days of delivery; we send a replacement first, or refund you if a replacement is not possible. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method in 5–10 business days.

In-stock items (paid in full, ship direct from our supplier)

IN-STOCK ITEM refund policy (paid in full, ships direct from our supplier): cancel any time before shipment for a full refund. Once shipped: no returns, refunds or exchanges. Exception: if your item arrives damaged, wrong or missing parts, email support@oliandcece.com with photos within 7 days of delivery; we send a replacement first, or refund you if a replacement is not possible. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method in 5–10 business days.

After shipment

Once an order has shipped, we do not accept returns, refunds or exchanges, except for the damaged, wrong or missing item cover below.

Damaged, wrong or missing items (7 days)

If your item arrives damaged, is the wrong item, or is missing parts, email support@oliandcece.com with your order number and photos within 7 days of delivery. We send a replacement first, or refund you if a replacement is not possible. This is the only exception to the no-returns rule for shipped orders.

Lost packages

No delivery scan 30 days after shipment: we send a replacement or a refund. See the Shipping Policy.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. They usually appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

Cost breakdown after production starts

If you cancel a custom order after confirming production start, we deduct only the costs we have actually incurred for your order (materials/parts, machining/production, freight) and send you an itemized breakdown. The deduction is never more than the deposit amount for that item; the rest is refunded.

How to cancel or claim

Email support@oliandcece.com with your order number (and photos for damaged/wrong items). Whether production had started is determined by your recorded Confirm production start click.
