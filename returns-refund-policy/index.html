Returns & Refund Policy

Cancellation and refund rules for Tachi Club deposit pre-orders, pay-in-full orders and in-stock items; 7-day damaged/wrong item cover.

Last updated 2026-11-01.

Deposit pre-orders (custom items)

Custom items are made/sourced to order. Your deposit is NON-REFUNDABLE once your item has been produced or shipped. You may cancel for a full deposit refund within 24 hours of placing your order. Cancellations after 24 hours (before production/shipping) incur a cancellation fee of 50% of the deposit; the remaining 50% is refunded. Exception: if your item arrives damaged, wrong or missing parts, email support@oliandcece.com with photos within 7 days of delivery for a replacement or refund. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method in 5–10 business days.

Paid-in-full custom items

PAY-IN-FULL refund policy (custom / made-to-order items): cancel within 24 hours of ordering for a full refund. After 24 hours and before shipment, cancellation is refunded 80% of the amount paid (20% cancellation fee). Once shipped: no returns, refunds or exchanges. Exception: if your item arrives damaged, wrong or missing parts, email support@oliandcece.com with photos within 7 days of delivery for a replacement or refund. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method in 5–10 business days.

In-stock items (paid in full, ship direct from our supplier)

IN-STOCK ITEM refund policy (paid in full, ships direct from our supplier): cancel within 24 hours of ordering for a full refund. After 24 hours and before shipment, cancellation is refunded 80% of the amount paid (20% cancellation fee). Once shipped: no returns, refunds or exchanges. Exception: if your item arrives damaged, wrong or missing parts, email support@oliandcece.com with photos within 7 days of delivery for a replacement or refund. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method in 5–10 business days.

After shipment

Once an order has shipped, we do not accept returns, refunds or exchanges, except for the damaged, wrong or missing item cover below.

Damaged, wrong or missing items (7 days)

If your item arrives damaged, is the wrong item, or is missing parts, email support@oliandcece.com with your order number and photos within 7 days of delivery. We will send a replacement or refund you. This is the only exception to the no-returns rule for shipped orders.

Lost packages

No delivery scan 30 days after shipment: we send a replacement or a refund. See the Shipping Policy.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. They usually appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

How to cancel or claim

Email support@oliandcece.com with your order number (and photos for damaged/wrong items). Cancellation timing is measured from the time your order was placed, as recorded in your order confirmation email.
