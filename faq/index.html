FAQ

Tachi Club sells made-to-order digital instrument clusters and carbon fiber parts for JDM and Porsche chassis, operated from Los Angeles (Commerce, CA).

Are these items in stock?

No. All items are custom / made to order (or sourced to order) for your car after fitment is confirmed.

How long does it take?

Current estimate: 3–5 weeks (estimate) after you approve the order. Some large items (e.g. the 718 rear diffuser) are estimated at 4–6 weeks. We will update you if this changes.

Do you ship outside the US?

No. We ship only to street addresses in the 50 US states and Washington, D.C. We do not ship to PO Boxes, APO/FPO/DPO, Puerto Rico or other US territories, or outside the US. US shipping is calculated at checkout based on your ZIP and package size; free local pickup at our Los Angeles warehouse. See https://tachiclub.oliandcece.com/shipping-policy/

How much is shipping?

US shipping is calculated at checkout based on your ZIP and package size; free local pickup at our Los Angeles warehouse. The shipping amount is shown as its own line before you pay (dadan carrier quote from Los Angeles to your ZIP). Deposit orders pay shipping with the balance; pay-in-full orders pay it at checkout. In-stock items that ship directly from our supplier include shipping in the price.

Is the deposit refundable? How do I cancel?

Deposit & cancellation policy: Custom items are made/sourced to order. After your deposit is paid we email you an order summary (vehicle, VIN, options, lead time) with a Confirm production start link; we do not order parts or start production until you confirm. Cancel before you confirm production start: full refund. Cancel after production/purchasing has started: we refund your deposit minus the actual costs already incurred for your order (materials, machining, freight), with an itemized cost breakdown; the deduction never exceeds your deposit. Once shipped: no returns, refunds or exchanges. Exception: if your item arrives damaged, wrong or missing parts, email support@oliandcece.com with photos within 7 days of delivery; we send a replacement first, or refund you if a replacement is not possible. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method in 5–10 business days.

What about my mileage (digital clusters)?

Federal law requires the replacement cluster to show the vehicle’s true mileage. We program each cluster to match your original odometer reading, which is why we ask for an odometer photo and VIN. We never alter mileage.

Will it fit my exact car?

We check your VIN, trim and photos of your current part before ordering or building.
